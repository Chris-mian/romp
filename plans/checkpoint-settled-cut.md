# Documents cut at the last settled turn (stage one b of the process split)

**Status:** design line, 2026-09-14, the manager's GO with five corrections folded in; the program is `plans/process-split.md`, the writer's walk `plans/checkpoint-cycle-walk.md` (stage one, merged). Written against upstream/main at f0a6c345. The line lands alone as a docs change; the code follows as one feature-tier pull request, red first per test under both interpreters.

## What is wrong

The assembly checkpoint writer cuts a leaf's document only at its last compaction boundary (`last_b`; a leaf with none is `noBoundary`, structural). So a session that never compacted has no document by design, the tail after the last compaction is decoded whole at every cold parse (the review measured 1.24 GB of the 4.5 GB in large transcripts), and sixteen live leaves stand refused for the tail's shape: their compaction boundary anchors, through its effective parent, on a uuid a later tail record reuses; last-wins resolves the anchor onto that later record and the tail's root region is a ring that every tail chain reaches, so no chain reaches the tip and the document cannot equal the cold parse. Any cut placed at that boundary refuses the same way; none of the sixteen leaves has moved since its mark.

## The rule

The cut is the boundary BEFORE the last SETTLED turn: a turn whose result record landed and whose next record exists, chosen at the settle, exactly where the writer runs today. The pre-cut spine walk is stage one's visited-set walk, so a ring in the pre-cut part resolves as the parse resolves it (repeated uuids last-wins, a self-link a root, the walk ending at the first revisit): the sixteen rings become pre-cut and their leaves get documents. The cut's guard bytes, offsets, files and prefix hash keep their shapes; the restore reads the tail from the cut as today; the record cache holds the tail only after a restore, as today, and readers hydrate pre-cut bodies through the lazy rows.

**The tail proof over a non-boundary tip (correction 1).** Today's graft predicate grew around the compaction boundary as the cut: the boundary's effective parent through `logicalParentUuid`, then the preserved segment; a tail record chains to the proven spine tip; `tipChildless` decided from the resolved graph at write time. With the cut at a settled turn's boundary the tip is a REGULAR record (the settled turn's last record), and the predicate requires: every tail record that bears a uuid or a parentUuid reaches the tip through TAIL parents, walked with a visited set (a revisit is a cycle and refuses, as today); the tip is childless of pre-cut children by construction, since every pre-cut record precedes it in the chain and a child of the tip is by definition past the cut, and the writer still decides and writes `tipChildless` from the resolved graph and the test pins it True for every settled-turn cut. A compaction boundary that now sits INSIDE the tail is held to the same rule through its effective parent, resolved as the parse resolves it (`logicalParentUuid`, else the preserved segment's tail, anchor or head that names a known record): anchored on the tip or a tail record it chains; anchored in the pre-cut interior or on no known record it refuses, as today. A tail uuid reusing a pre-cut record's refuses, as today (round seven); a uuid repeated within the tail is the parse's last-wins node.

**The churn bound (correction 2).** The cut advances only when the tail since the standing cut has grown past ONE EIGHTH of the pre-cut bytes, or a compaction landed; otherwise the standing document stands and the settle writes nothing. The basis: each rewrite costs a whole parse of the leaf, so a share bounds the rewrites over a leaf's life to a logarithm of its growth (a leaf that doubles rewrites three times) while the tail every cold parse still decodes stays under an eighth of the documented part; the eighth is the share the machine's own caps use (a capped job's CPU and memory quota), not a literal chosen for this file. A compaction is the other event because the boundary changes what the tail is.

**A tail that never settles.** The cut never chases a live turn. A session mid-turn for hours keeps its previous document: the cut stands at the boundary before the open turn began, the open turn's records are the tail, decoded at every cold parse as today, and nothing is written while the turn is open, since the settle is the event; when the turn settles, the next settle may advance the cut past it. A cut at a settled turn boundary never has an open turn at its tip, which closes the low the stage-one read queued (a tail grafted onto an open-turn tip restoring as a new turn where the cold parse joins it).

**The standing refusal mark when the cut moves (correction 3).** A `refusedStanding` mark belongs to a cut; when the rewrite lands a document with a new cut, the mark for the cut that no longer exists is retired with the rewrite, its sidecar bytes kept as forensics the way the flags quarantine keeps its sidecar (renamed, never deleted), so the next restore proves the new document rather than standing down on the old mark.

**The version term (correction 4).** The document's version `_ASM_CKPT_V` is bumped from 6 to 7 for the new cut rule (the persisted-memo rule: a version term bumped on a logic change), so every standing document is refused ONCE at the deploy boot (`version`, counted) and rewritten at the next settle. That boot's whole parses are therefore expected to equal the number of documents standing at the boot, once (on the devbox about two dozen), and the pull request's body says so, so nobody reads them as a regression; the boot after it is the measurement.

## Correctness

The restore-equality oracle over a MOVING cut: every compacting golden scenario and every scenario made to compact is cut at EACH settled turn boundary in turn (a sweep over cut positions), and restore-then-hydrate must equal the cold parse at every position; the ring fixtures of stage one at a cut past the ring. One adversarial shape per class the earlier rounds found, each restore-then-hydrate equal to the cold parse (correction 5): a repeated uuid across the cut; a self-link at the tip; a rewind from the tail onto a pre-cut record; a `/clear` fork in the tail; a compaction boundary in the tail anchored on a pre-cut record. Red first per test at the base under both interpreters.
